# Drop your recorded end-to-end demo here

Put a screen recording in this folder and `build_deck.py` will auto-embed the **first**
video it finds on the DEMO slide.

Supported: `.mp4`, `.m4v`, `.mov`, `.webm`, `.avi`  ·  suggested ~3–4 min, 1080p H.264.

Suggested beats: (1) opt in & publish → (2) backend threads the build across two clouds
→ (3) open the share link & stream.
